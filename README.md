## Current Work

### The Digital Self — SSI Platform on ICP
An independent sovereign-identity platform built on the Internet Computer. The core argument: digital sovereignty is three compounding layers — credential sovereignty (owning your verified identity), economic sovereignty (owning the value your behavior produces), and epistemic sovereignty (owning the lens through which you encounter the world).

Whitepaper draft: [Whitepaper.md](https://github.com/Jack0thy/Digital-Self/blob/main/docs/Whitepaper.md)

Sovereignty Layers; in development

### Signet Provenance
Decentralized infrastructure for cultural institutions to register and steward artifact provenance on-chain — museums, fossils, and cultural heritage objects, with tamper-proof custody records and researcher access managed through a ledger. *(Formerly Heritage Ledger.)*
